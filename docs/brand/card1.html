01The problem

A signature on the code,
and on the AI claim.

A pull request arrives from someone you don't know. A person, or increasingly an AI agent. Trust rests on three things: a GitHub username, the platform, and reading the diff by eye.

SCPE replaces the first two with something you can check yourself: a signed envelope proving who signed a contribution and that nothing was tampered with. Verified with no SCPE server and no new accounts.
